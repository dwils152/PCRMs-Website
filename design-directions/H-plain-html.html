PCRMs: Predicted cis-Regulatory Modules

A database of predicted cis-regulatory modules and their constituent transcription factor binding sites.

Home | Browse | Closest CRMs | CRMs around a gene | TF binding sites | Downloads | Cite | Su Lab

PCRMs holds CRMs and transcription factor binding sites (TFBSs) predicted de novo from public TF ChIP-seq data. The version 2 human map has 1,426,947 CRMs and 95,717,222 TFBSs (85.5% of the genome); the version 2 mouse map has 912,197 CRMs and 125,113,756 TFBSs (77.9% of the genome). Version 1 maps are available for C. elegans, A. thaliana and D. melanogaster.

Other searches

  • Closest CRMs to a gene — CRMs whose closest gene matches a symbol or ID (e.g. RUNX1, ENSG00000159216).
  • CRMs around a gene — upstream, downstream or both sides, within 0.5, 1 or 2 Mb.
  • TF binding sites — predicted sites for a TF symbol on chosen chromosomes.
