PCRMs: predicted cis-regulatory modules and their constituent transcription factor binding sites

Five genomes · Version 2 maps for human and mouse · Latest release 3 June 2022

Abstract

PCRMs is a database of cis-regulatory modules (CRMs) and transcription factor binding sites (TFBSs) predicted de novo by integrating public TF ChIP-seq datasets across cell and tissue types. The version 2 maps cover 85.5% of the human genome with 1,426,947 CRMs and 95,717,222 TFBSs from 11,348 datasets, and 77.9% of the mouse genome with 912,197 CRMs and 125,113,756 TFBSs from 9,060 datasets. Version 1 maps are available for C. elegans, A. thaliana and D. melanogaster.

2. Analysis tools

2.1 Closest CRMs to a gene. Enter a gene symbol or stable identifier, for example RUNX1 or ENSG00000159216, to list the predicted CRMs whose closest gene it is, filtered by P value. Open this search

2.2 CRMs around a gene. Search upstream, downstream or on both sides of a gene within 0.5, 1 or 2 Mb. Upstream and downstream follow the gene’s annotated strand. Open this search

2.3 Transcription factor binding sites. Find predicted binding sites for a TF symbol on selected chromosomes, with motif identifiers, sequence logos for the human, mouse and C. elegans collections, and links back to each CRM. Open this search

3. Downloads

Genome-wide CRM and TFBS coordinates are distributed as BED files in tar.gz archives, one per organism and release. Version 2 mouse CRMs are also provided lifted over to GRCm39/mm39. Go to downloads
