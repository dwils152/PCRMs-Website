PCRMs: Predicted cis-Regulatory Modules

A database of predicted CRMs and their constituent transcription factor binding sites in five genomes

About PCRMs

PCRMs contains cis-regulatory modules (CRMs) and their constituent transcription factor binding sites (TFBSs), predicted de novo by integrating available TF ChIP-seq datasets across cell and tissue types with dePCRM2.
