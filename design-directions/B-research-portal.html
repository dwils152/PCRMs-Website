Predicted cis-regulatory modules and transcription factor binding sites

PCRMs holds CRMs and their constituent TFBSs predicted de novo from public TF ChIP-seq data. Version 2 maps cover 85.5% of the human genome and 77.9% of the mouse genome; version 1 maps are available for C. elegans, A. thaliana and D. melanogaster.

Analysis tools

Closest CRMs to a gene
CRMs whose closest gene matches a symbol or stable ID, filtered by P value.
CRMs around a gene
Strand-aware search upstream, downstream or on both sides within 0.5, 1 or 2 Mb.
TF binding sites
Predicted sites for a TF on selected chromosomes, with motif IDs, logos and parent CRMs.
