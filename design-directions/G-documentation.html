Getting started / Overview

Overview

PCRMs is a database of cis-regulatory modules (CRMs) and their constituent transcription factor binding sites (TFBSs), predicted de novo with dePCRM2 from public TF ChIP-seq data. You can query it on the website or download genome-wide BED files.

Quick start

  1. Browse a chromosome. Choose an organism, chromosomes and a P-value threshold (0.05, 0.01, 5 × 10⁻⁶ or 1 × 10⁻⁶). Results are paginated on the server, so whole chromosomes load quickly.
  2. Open a CRM. Each record shows coordinates, score, P value and closest genes, with the region in the NCBI sequence viewer.
  3. Inspect its binding sites. The TFBS table lists positions, binding scores, motif IDs, motif logos where available and matched TFs, and exports to CSV or Excel.
  4. Start from a gene or TF instead. Use Closest CRMs, CRMs around a gene or TF binding sites.

Working with the BED files
