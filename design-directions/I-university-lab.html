About the database

PCRMs holds cis-regulatory modules (CRMs) and their constituent transcription factor binding sites (TFBSs), predicted de novo by integrating public TF ChIP-seq datasets across cell and tissue types with the dePCRM2 method.

The version 2 maps cover 85.5% of the human genome and 77.9% of the mouse genome, built from 11,348 and 9,060 ChIP-seq datasets. Version 1 maps are available for C. elegans, A. thaliana and D. melanogaster.

Analysis tools

Closest CRMs to a gene
CRMs whose closest gene matches a symbol or stable ID, filtered by P value.
CRMs around a gene
Upstream, downstream or both sides within 0.5, 1 or 2 Mb, following the gene’s strand.
TF binding sites
Predicted sites for a TF symbol on chosen chromosomes, with motif logos and parent CRMs.
