Description

Cis-regulatory modules (CRMs) and their constituent transcription factor binding sites (TFBSs), predicted de novo by integrating public TF ChIP-seq datasets across cell and tissue types. Version 2 covers 85.5% of the human genome (1,426,947 CRMs, 95,717,222 TFBSs, 11,348 datasets) and 77.9% of the mouse genome (912,197 CRMs, 125,113,756 TFBSs, 9,060 datasets). Version 1 maps are included for C. elegans, A. thaliana and D. melanogaster.
